# Aurum Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Aurum forecasts how good tonight's sunset will be. This page explains what leaves your phone and why.

## Weather forecasts
To forecast a place, Aurum sends that place's coordinates to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), a weather service. It also sends the coordinates of points up to 300 km along the direction of the sunset, to read the clouds the light passes through. In the hours before tonight's sunset it also sends the place rounded to about 5 km, with points up to 500 km toward the sunset and to either side of it, and asks for the height of the ground around it, to collect the extra weather a newer version of the forecast will use. Like any web request, this lets Open-Meteo see your IP address. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy applies to those requests. Aurum has no account system.

## Your sunset log
Your notes always stay on your phone. Your ratings stay on your phone unless you turn on sharing (below). Aurum also keeps each forecast it makes for an evening on your phone, so a rating can be compared with what it said in the days before. Forecasts for evenings you did not rate are deleted after 30 days.

## Sharing ratings (off unless you turn it on)
If you choose "Share ratings", Aurum sends each rating with:
- the score you gave and the evening it was for,
- how many days ahead the forecast was made,
- the forecast's score, its three parts (Canvas: cloud for the light to paint; Light path: sunlight reaching that cloud; Clarity: how clean the air is) and the weather inputs it was based on (cloud at points toward the sunset, humidity, visibility, dust and haze, recent rain),
- for the evening itself, the extra weather collected for the newer forecast at the place rounded to about 5 km: cloud from several weather models at points toward and either side of the sunset, cloud and humidity at different heights, the depth of the hazy air near the ground, dust, haze and fine particles, and how high the hills rise toward the sunset,
- the earlier forecasts for that evening: each one's score and three parts, with how many hours before sunset it was made (never the time of day),
- the formula version,
- the location rounded to about 5 km,
- the app version,
- a random ID created on your phone, so you can delete what you shared.

It never sends your notes, place names, the time you logged, your exact location, your name, an account or any device identifier. The only ID is the random one above, made on your phone and not linked to you. Like any web request, the server sees your IP address, which is not stored with the ratings.

**Why:** only to measure and improve the forecast. The data is never sold, shared for advertising, or combined with other data.

**Where:** stored with Supabase in the European Union.

**How long:** until you delete it, and at most 3 years: older ratings are deleted automatically.

**Your choices:** turn sharing off at any time in Settings. "Delete my shared ratings" in Settings removes everything your phone has shared. If you deleted the app without doing that, email lukefarah22@gmail.com: without the random ID, ratings can only be found roughly by date and area, so say which evenings and roughly where.
