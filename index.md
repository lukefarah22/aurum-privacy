# Aurum Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Aurum forecasts how good tonight's sunset will be. This page explains what leaves your phone and why.

## Weather forecasts
To forecast a place, Aurum sends that place's coordinates to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), a weather service. It also sends the coordinates of points up to 300 km along the direction of the sunset, to read the clouds the light passes through. Like any web request, this lets Open-Meteo see your IP address. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy applies to those requests. Aurum has no account system.

## Your sunset log
Your notes always stay on your phone. Your ratings stay on your phone unless you turn on sharing (below).

## Sharing ratings (off unless you turn it on)
If you choose "Share ratings", Aurum sends each rating with:
- the score you gave and the evening it was for,
- how many days ahead the forecast was made,
- the forecast's score, its three parts (cloud to light up, light path, air clarity) and the weather inputs it was based on (cloud at points toward the sunset, humidity, visibility, dust and haze, recent rain),
- the formula version,
- the location rounded to about 5 km,
- the app version,
- a random ID created on your phone, so you can delete what you shared.

It never sends your notes, place names, the time you logged, your exact location, your name, an account or any device identifier. The only ID is the random one above, made on your phone and not linked to you. Like any web request, the server sees your IP address, which is not stored with the ratings.

**Why:** only to measure and improve the forecast. The data is never sold, shared for advertising, or combined with other data.

**Where:** stored with Supabase in the European Union.

**How long:** until you delete it, and at most 3 years: older ratings are deleted automatically.

**Your choices:** turn sharing off at any time in Settings. "Delete my shared ratings" in Settings removes everything your phone has shared. If you deleted the app without doing that, email lukefarah22@gmail.com: without the random ID, ratings can only be found roughly by date and area, so say which evenings and roughly where.
